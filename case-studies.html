FinTech Startup: Multi-Billion Parameter Model

Industry: Financial Services / Company Size: 50 people

A Series A fintech was planning to train a 70B parameter model on their proprietary financial data. Their team had extensive ML expertise but no LLM-specific architecture experience.

Issues Found

  • Hidden dimension (8192) incompatible with their target GPUs
  • Attention heads (64) not divisible by hidden dim
  • Estimated training cost: $145K — 2x their budget

Outcome: Recommended a 13B model with architectural fixes. Same capability, $60K training cost. Client redirected budget to data quality improvements instead.

Fixed 3 Critical Bugs

AI Platform: Production Inference API

Industry: AI Infrastructure / Company Size: 200 people

An established AI platform was seeing intermittent inference failures in production. Their team had been debugging for 3 weeks with no root cause identified.

Issues Found

  • Hidden dimension misaligned with vocab size causing padding issues
  • Position encoding overflow for sequences > 4096 tokens
  • FFN intermediate dimension violated GELU constraints

Outcome: ArchGene identified all 3 issues in 30 minutes. Provided fixes for each. Production now stable.
